Weekly Financial Tips for Everyday Growth

The 24-Hour Rule

Before any non-essential purchase over $100, wait 24 hours. If you still want it, buy it.

02

Target High-APR Debts First

Paying off a card with 22% APR yields a guaranteed 22% return. Avoid low-yield savings until high debt is gone.

03

Automate Your Savings Transfer

Treat savings like a bill. Route 10% of your paycheck directly into savings or index portfolios.
